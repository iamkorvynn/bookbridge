# BookBridge — Play Store listing

## Short description (80 chars max)

Buy & sell used textbooks on your campus. List, browse, WhatsApp the seller.

## Long description

BookBridge is the campus textbook exchange. Finished with a book? List it
in a minute — snap a photo, add the title, subject, price and condition,
and it is up for sale to students on your campus.

Looking for a book? Browse every listing with photos and prices, search by
title, author or subject, filter by subject or condition, and sort by price
or newest first. Found your book? Tap WhatsApp to message the seller
directly with a prefilled message, or call them — no middleman, no
commission.

When a book sells, mark it as sold. Your listings live in My listings,
where you can manage or delete them any time.

Everything is stored on your own device — no account, no server, no
tracking. BookBridge starts empty: every listing on it is a real book
from a real student.

## Feature bullets

- List a textbook in a minute with a photo, price and condition
- Browse, search and filter by subject, author or condition
- Sort by price or newest first
- Contact sellers directly on WhatsApp or by call
- Mark books as sold, manage everything in My listings
- Works fully offline — all data stored on your device, no account needed
